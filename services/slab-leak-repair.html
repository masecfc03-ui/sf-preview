What's Involved

Slab leaks can destroy a foundation if left untreated. We locate the leak with pressure testing, open only what needs opening, and repair it right the first time.

How Long Does It Take?

Leak detection takes 1–2 hours. Repair time depends on the location — simple fixes are same-day, larger jobs may take 1–2 days.

What Happens If You Wait?

Small plumbing problems almost always turn into bigger ones. A slow drain becomes a complete blockage. A small leak works on your foundation and your drywall. If you're not sure whether something needs attention now or can wait, call us — we'll tell you straight.
